# Legal and Compliance Review Checklist

Code controls do not equal legal compliance. Legal and operational review is required before public marketing.

- [ ] Marketing consent copy approved.
- [ ] Consent-text versioning approved.
- [ ] CAN-SPAM mailing address approved.
- [ ] Email unsubscribe wording approved.
- [ ] SMS opt-in wording approved.
- [ ] SMS HELP/STOP wording approved.
- [ ] Twilio A2P 10DLC registration approved.
- [ ] Quiet-hour policy approved by market.
- [ ] Data retention schedule approved.
- [ ] Privacy Policy updated for campaign analytics.
- [ ] Terms updated for partner communications.
- [ ] Restaurant partner terms prohibit guest-list misuse.
- [ ] Incident process approved for mistaken campaign sends.
- [ ] Suppression-list operational SLA defined.
- [ ] Cross-border data transfer review completed where applicable.
