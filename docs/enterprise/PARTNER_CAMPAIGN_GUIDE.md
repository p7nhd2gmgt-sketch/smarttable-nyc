# Partner Campaign Guide

Partner campaigns are restaurant-scoped marketing tools.

## Eligible Audiences

Partners may target only users connected to their restaurant, such as:

- users who favorited or followed the restaurant;
- users with accepted or completed reservations at that restaurant;
- future restaurant-specific subscribers with valid consent.

## Not Allowed

Partners must not receive raw exported SmartTable guest lists. They cannot target another restaurant's audience.

## Workflow

1. Create draft.
2. Add EN/ES/HU content as needed.
3. Estimate audience.
4. Send a diagnostic test to an explicit address.
5. Schedule or send now.
6. Monitor aggregate delivery status.
7. Clone, archive, cancel, or retry failed deliveries where eligible.

## Content Safety

Campaign bodies are sanitized server-side. Template variables are restricted to a safe allowlist. Passwords, tokens, private preference fields, and unsupported internals cannot be used as template variables.
